01 Overview & Guest Gameplay

X Robot Runner ("we", "our", or "the Game") is designed to respect player privacy. You can play X Robot Runner immediately in Guest Mode without creating an account, providing your real name, or signing in with an email address.

02 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To keep your game progress working smoothly offline and between sessions, the Game stores the following data locally on your device using Android Room database and private SharedPreferences:

  • Selected Pilot Name / Callsign, active robot skin, active world/background, and fire effect preferences
  • High score, total distance run, matches played, and audio/vibration settings
  • Virtual currency balance (XC Credits) and unlocked in-game items
  • Local Google Play purchase receipt summaries (Order ID, product ID, timestamp, and deduplication token hash) to prevent duplicate delivery

We do not sell your personal information, serve third-party behavioral advertising, or collect precise GPS location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, or camera feeds.

03 Google Play Billing & In-App Purchases

Optional XC Credit packs and digital bundles inside X Robot Runner are processed exclusively through Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your credit card number, bank account, or Google Play payment credentials.

When you complete a purchase, Google Play provides purchase confirmation details (such as the Google Play Order ID, product ID, purchase state, and purchase token) so the Game and our verification system can validate your transaction, prevent replay/fraud, and restore eligible purchases.

04 Network Connectivity

The Game checks basic internet connectivity status (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE / INTERNET) solely to connect to Google Play Billing services for in-app purchases and purchase restoration. No mandatory cloud login is required to play.

05 Data Retention & Deletion Requests

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall X Robot Runner (standard Android Auto Backup settings may apply if enabled on your device).

If server-side Google Play order verification records are processed for fraud prevention or refund reconciliation, you may request deletion or support at any time by emailing shihabba2312@gmail.com with your Google Play Order ID.

06 Children's Privacy

X Robot Runner does not knowingly collect personal contact information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal data to us via support email, please contact shihabba2312@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.

07 XC Credit Terms of Service

  • Virtual Currency Only: XC Credits ("XC") are a digital in-game virtual currency licensed solely for unlocking digital items, skins, worlds, effects, and revives within X Robot Runner.
  • No Monetary Value: XC Credits have no real-world monetary value, cannot be redeemed for cash or legal tender, and are non-transferable outside your game profile.
  • Refunds & Revocation: All purchases are governed by Google Play's refund policies. If a Google Play purchase is refunded, voided, or charged back, corresponding unspent XC Credits or unlocked digital entitlements may be revoked.
  • Offline & Demo Notice: Any offline demo leaderboard displayed in guest mode represents local comparison scores and does not publish your identity online.

08 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy & Terms as new online features (such as optional Google Sign-In or live cloud leaderboards) are introduced. Updates will be reflected on this page.
